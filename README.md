# US 100-Bagger Census, 1986 to 2026

A study in the spirit of Thomas Phelps's *100 to 1 in the Stock Market* (1972): which US stocks could have been bought between 1986 and 2025 for 1/100 or less of their 2026 high, how long each buying window stayed open, and how the list compares with Phelps's 1932 to 1971 list.

Live page: https://esperansa-spec.github.io/100-bagger-census/

`index.html` is self-contained (data and charting code inlined). Sources and method are described at the bottom of the page. This is a historical study and is not investment advice.
